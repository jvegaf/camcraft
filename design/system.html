Campilot — design system

Twenty-one roles, eight text styles and one geometry, rendered exactly as crates/cam-ui/src/theme.rs defines them, over the shell camui-prancheta delivered: a 200 px rail of five folding groups, two 22 px rulers, a ribbon and a task editor on the right, and a status bar in three zones. A widget never names a colour — it asks Palette::get(Role::…), so both sets re-resolve from the same list.

The rail at 200 px, one group opened and the next folded, over the 62 px brand block. The words are the group headings; a chip is a glyph and nothing else — 20 px on the 1.6 stroke, and its label is the tooltip (Select, Line, Rectangle, …), which is why the palette is the part of the frame that carries no text of its own. No chip is ever drawn disabled: all twenty-eight are pressable, because what a command can do is decided when it runs and reported in the status bar. A heading is a 14 px row the operator folds, and the caret is painted rather than written. A chip is 32 × 32, radius 4; idle is Text on Panel, hover fills Raised, active is the SelSoft wash under a 1 px Sel edge. No letter is drawn next to a glyph — no rail command has a single-key shortcut.

05 Rules design/system.md

Mandatory

  • Contrast is proven, not asserted. Twelve pairs the shell writes on clear AA body (4.5) or AA large (3.0) in both sets — TextHi on App/Panel, Text on Panel/Canvas, TextDim on App/Panel, Sel and Cut on Panel/Canvas, Ok and Warn on Panel.
  • The luminance ladder holds — dark Canvas < App < Panel < Raised < Overlay, light App < Raised < Canvas < Panel.
  • Selection and cutting never share a hue: at least 60° apart, and they differ in shape too — Sel is a dashed-then-solid outline with square handles, Cut is 1.6 px with a round cap. A monochrome print still tells them apart.
  • The stock reads as a solid: every stop on the ramp clears 1.45:1 against the canvas, StockBottom is always darker than StockTop, and StockEdge is held to 3:1 against both the canvas and the stock's own top.
  • A 2 px Sel focus ring outside every interactive element, so it never moves what it points at.
  • Motion is ≤ 140 ms and prefers-reduced-motion: reduce turns every transition and animation off.
  • A unit once per column, a tooltip on every icon-only control, and a sentence — never a dead control — when an action cannot run.

Forbidden

  • A second accent. There is Sel and there is Cut; a third means the screen is telling two stories.
  • A filled icon beside a stroked one — the set is stroke-only at 1.6, and a glyph's mixed parts are dashes and washes, never a heavier pen.
  • More than three control heights. 24 is the control, 26 the row, and a bar is its own thing.
  • A shadow on a docked surface. Only the modal and the overlay cast one.
  • Colour as the only carrier of meaning, at any level.
  • A number without its unit, or a unit on every row of one column.
  • An unnamed value. If it is not in this board or in theme.rs, it is not a token.
