Rules

Mandatory

· Every pair the shell writes on meets AA (4.5:1); strokes and signals meet 3:1 — proven by test, not by eye.

· Every interactive element has a 2 px focus ring on both sets.

· Every icon-only control carries a tooltip with its name and key.

· Numeric fields align right on tabular figures, unit in a fixed column.

Forbidden

· A second accent hue, or a filled variant beside the line icon set.

· Shadows on docked surfaces, or a border weight other than 1 px.

· Colour as the only carrier of meaning.

· Any gap that is not on the spacing scale, any numeric field without a unit.
