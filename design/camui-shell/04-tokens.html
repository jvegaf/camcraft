Campilot — visual tokens

The reference app's charm is not decoration: it is a graphite canvas, one line weight, and two signals that never compete. Geometry you touch is cyan; material the tool will remove is amber. Everything else is neutral. These are the values crates/cam-ui/src/theme.rs resolves, and the shell they dress is the one camui-prancheta delivered: a rail of five folding groups over a brand block, two rulers, a ribbon and a task editor on the right, three zones in the status bar.

02 The two signals, and the one gradient Sel vs Cut; the stock ramp

Selection — cyan

--sel #6fd3e8. Picked vectors, the active toggle, the active rail chip, the ribbon's open category, the focus ring, the TOP axis. Drawn as a dashed-then-solid outline with square handles, so a monochrome print still reads it as a selection.

--sel-soft rgba(105,202,225,.13) — the wash under a selected chip or row, drawn straight onto the panel
Cutting — amber

--cut #f5a623. The ruler marker, the toolpath preview, the depth a pass reaches, and the one primary action. Drawn at 1.6 px with a round cap — a different shape from a selection, not only a different colour.

--cut-soft rgba(240,165,30,.13) — the wash under the primary action and the cut chip

Why they can never be confused: the two sit at least 60° apart in hue, and the shell proves it in a test rather than trusting the eye. A third accent is the end of the argument — with two signals every mark means one of two things, and with three it means whichever one you guess. On paper the same two roles are #0d7c92 and #a86200: a cyan that reads on #0d1014 disappears on #f7f9fb, so the light set darkens both signals until they clear AA against its canvas.

Ok — a pass

#5ec27f. A verification came back clean, the tool resolved, the program was written. The RIGHT axis of the triad.

Warn — unwise

#f0b429. Legal and still a bad idea: a tool wider than the slot, a depth past the stock. A number the shell will not trust.

Danger — a refusal

#f2635f. A gouge, a crash, an open vector where a closed one is required. Always paired with words. The FRONT axis.

The stock ramp — the only gradient in the shell

Material is the one thing that shades, because it is the one thing being cut: a lit top face into a darker floor, with the edge a role of its own rather than a darker mix.

dark #d6dce6 → #424c58, edge #6e7b8a
light #c6d1dd → #a6b3c3, edge #4a5563

The ramp is stock_shade(t), which clamps t to 0..=1: -1 and 2 return the ends and NaN returns the top. Every stop on it — StockTop and StockBottom alike — is held to at least 1.45:1 against the canvas, because the material is a solid and not a word: 3:1 would make the stock darker than the cut signal it carries. StockBottom is always darker than StockTop and never the canvas itself, and StockEdge clears 3:1 against both the canvas and the stock's own top, so a cut edge stays legible even where the fill is page-light. Nothing else in the shell interpolates — a gradient anywhere else is decoration pretending to be state.

Numbers are tabular and set in the same face: a machinist compares a column down its decimals, so a figure that changes width as it changes value is a figure that moves under the eye.

Numbers are furniture — the unit appears once per column

The unit is a column heading, not a suffix on every row. Repeating mm five times is five pieces of noise in front of one fact.

App bar — idle, 40 px
campilot bracket-plate.c2d · modified mm

The bar carries the document and the display; the primary action is disabled while there is nothing to emit, and the sentence in its tooltip says why. No command lives here that the rail also offers — one act, one place.

Rail chip — idle, hover, active
A chip is a glyph and nothing else — 20 px at 1.6 stroke in a 32 × 32 target, the command's label carried in the tooltip, because the words of this rail are its group headings. Idle is Text on Panel, hover fills Raised, active is the SelSoft wash under a 1 px Sel edge, and the glyph keeps its weight. 06 What this rules out each one is a drift, not a taste

  • A second accent There is Sel and there is Cut. A hue that means “this panel's active thing” is a third story, and from then on no colour means anything on its own.
  • A filled icon beside a stroked one The set is stroke-only at 1.6 in every state, hover included. A glyph's mixed parts are dashes and 22 % washes, never a heavier pen.
  • More than three control heights 24 is the control, 26 the row, and a bar is its own thing. A fourth height is a fourth rhythm nobody can unsee.
  • A shadow on a docked surface Hierarchy between docked surfaces is one hairline. Only the tool library's modal and the 3D overlay cast a shadow, because only they float.
  • Colour as the only carrier of meaning Selection and cutting differ in hue and in shape; a refusal is a sentence starting refused: before it is red. A monochrome print still says what happened.
  • A number without its unit — and equally, a unit repeated on every row of one column. The unit lives in the column heading, in the working-unit field of the app bar, and beside the coordinates in the status bar.
